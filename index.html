WHO WE ARE

Bringing dairy goodness
closer to you.

MADDO DAIRIES is a catholic based organised started in early 1990 by the Hia Lordship the Bishop of Masaka Diocese Rt Rev Bp John Baptist Kaggwa. presented as a customer-focused dairy business committed to making quality dairy products accessible to households and organisations.

Our website showcases a range of dairy products, clear product information and a simple way for customers to make enquiries. We believe that good service begins with care, consistency and respect for every customer.
